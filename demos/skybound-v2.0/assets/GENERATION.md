# Generated texture assets

Created with the built-in image generation tool for this local SKYBOUND Japan demo. Original generated files are retained; these workspace copies are the build inputs. Final images are reduced without dithering into a shared 256-entry palette (5-bit RGB hardware channels).

- japan-materials-generated.png: 4x2 material atlas: grass, modern facade, traditional timber/plaster, temple roof, volcanic rock, snow, stone, water. Soft illumination and low noise.
- cockpit-instruments-generated.png: four analog training-aircraft instrument faces, attitude / speed / altitude / heading. Decorative artwork, not certified instrument scales.
- sky-generated.png: blue sky and delicate cirrus, no terrain or painted horizon.
- ground-generated.png: four orthographic overhead tiles: Japanese city blocks, rice paddies, Kyoto roofs and gardens, forest. Uniform light, readable broad forms, no labels.
- fuji-generated.png: continuous cylindrical mountain surface, snowy summit top, slate volcanic slopes below, muted forest foot; no sky or silhouette, seamless horizontal edges. Mapped continuously around the polygon mountain instead of repeating a whole rock patch on every face.

The image generation prompts requested original assets, no logos, and legibility at small texture resolution. Generated artwork is not geographic survey data.
